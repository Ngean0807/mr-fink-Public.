## Presentation notes raised and how they were addressed

1. Movers exclude splits, listing changes, spin-offs and acquisitions: the rule is now stated on the card.
2. ETF holdings added up by issuer (TLT's 46 Treasury bonds, Alphabet's two share classes): each grouped line now says how many lines it combines and is no longer linked to a single security.
3. ETF returns are price returns: the column headers and footnote say so.
4. ETF fund size is the whole fund across share classes: the column header and footnote say so.
5. EPS TTM uses TTM net income over the latest diluted share count (7.94); the sum of reported quarterly EPS is 7.91. Both are standard; the site's method is unchanged.
6. BlackRock's book total excludes option rows ($16.96B), as do all totals on the site (long share positions only).
